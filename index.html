About Mekhel Powell: Cybersecurity and Networking Journey

I am a second year graduate student pursuing my M.S. in Cybersecurity and Privacy at the Georgia Institute of Technology. From solving coursework CTF challenges to deploying honeypots and analyzing telemetry in my homelab, I focus on understanding both attack patterns and mitigation techniques.
