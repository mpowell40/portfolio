About Me

About Mekhel Powell: Cybersecurity and Networking Journey

I am a second year graduate student pursuing my M.S. in Cybersecurity and Privacy at the Georgia Institute of Technology. Building upon a strong foundation in network architecture and protocols, my work focuses on understanding how systems communicate from the ground up, and how to defend them against modern threats.

From solving coursework CTF challenges to deploying honeypots and analyzing telemetry in my homelab, I focus on understanding both attack patterns and mitigation techniques. Maintaining clear technical documentation across these environments is key to my work today and supports my long-term goal of teaching and mentoring emerging security talent later in my career.
